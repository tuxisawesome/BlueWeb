This is a clone of the game [Block Blast](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle&hl=en_US) for TI-84+ CE calculators. It's like Tetris, but the blocks don't fall, and you can clear lines vertically. Basically, you have to fit random pieces into an 8x8 grid until you can't anymore.

## Controls
Arrow keys - Move your current piece
2nd        - Place your current piece
Alpha      - Swap your current and next pieces
